# Maharashtra Traffic Rules and Compounding Fines (Amendments)

This document contains the official compounding fees, fines, and penalties for traffic violations in Maharashtra, implemented under the Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act.

## Two-Wheeler Violations

### Riding Without a Helmet (Protective Headgear)
- **Applicability:** Applies to both the rider and the pillion passenger.
- **Section:** Section 129 read with Section 194D of the Motor Vehicles Act.
- **Penalty / Fine:** **₹500 (Five Hundred Rupees)**.
- **License Action:** Disqualification and suspension of the driving license for a period of **3 months (three months)**.
- **Rules:** The helmet must be ISI-marked (BIS-certified) and must be securely fastened with straps to the wearer's head.

### Carrying More Than One Person (Triple Riding)
- **Section:** Section 128 read with Section 194C of the Motor Vehicles Act.
- **Penalty / Fine:** **₹1,000 (One Thousand Rupees)**.
- **License Action:** Disqualification and suspension of the driving license for a period of **3 months (three months)**.

## General Driving Violations

### Driving Without a Valid Driving License
- **Section:** Section 3/4 read with Section 181 of the Motor Vehicles Act.
- **Penalty / Fine:** **₹5,000 (Five Thousand Rupees)**.

### Driving at Excessive Speed (Speeding / Speed Limit Violation)
- **Section:** Section 112 read with Section 183 of the Motor Vehicles Act.
- **Penalty / Fine:** 
  - Light Motor Vehicles (Cars/Jeeps): **₹1,000 to ₹2,000**.
  - Medium Goods/Passenger Vehicles or Heavy Motor Vehicles: **₹2,000 to ₹4,000** (for first offence) and **₹4,000 to ₹8,000** (for second or subsequent offence).

### Drunken Driving (Driving Under the Influence of Alcohol or Drugs)
- **Section:** Section 185 of the Motor Vehicles Act.
- **Penalty / Fine:** **₹10,000 (Ten Thousand Rupees)** and/or imprisonment up to 6 months for the first offence; **₹15,000 (Fifteen Thousand Rupees)** and/or imprisonment up to 2 years for a second or subsequent offence.

### Jumping Traffic Signals (Red Light Violation)
- **Section:** Section 119 read with Section 177/177A of the Motor Vehicles Act.
- **Penalty / Fine:** **₹500 (Five Hundred Rupees)**.

### Using Mobile Phone While Driving (Dangerous Driving)
- **Section:** Section 184(c) of the Motor Vehicles Act.
- **Penalty / Fine:** 
  - First Offence: **₹1,000 to ₹5,000** or imprisonment from 6 months to 1 year.
  - Subsequent Offence (within 3 years): **₹10,000 (Ten Thousand Rupees)** or imprisonment up to 2 years.

### Driving Without a Valid Seat Belt
- **Section:** Section 194B of the Motor Vehicles Act.
- **Penalty / Fine:** **₹200 (Two Hundred Rupees)**.
